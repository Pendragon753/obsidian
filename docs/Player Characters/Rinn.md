Rinn is a half-elf who never knew either of her parents, the human or the elf. She was found as a baby in the forest outside a halfling village and was adopted by the village. Raised by a halfling couple, she gained a love and appreciation for nature and living with the land. She learned how to wield a bow in defense of livestock and the village, protecting the forest and village around them from danger. When an insidious magic started creeping towards the village, turning the trees black, she left the village in search of answers, promising not to return until she could stop the darkness from spreading.

Friendly with most characters
